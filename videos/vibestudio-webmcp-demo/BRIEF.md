## Intent

A concise product showcase for the WebMCP Challenge. Lead with the frustrating reset that creators experience in general-purpose chat, then show VibeStudio's real shared workspace and real Codex WebMCP actions. The product should feel calm, capable, and unusually collaborative—not like another AI chat wrapper.

## Assets

- ../../docs/hackathon-build/evidence/inspire-final-cropped.png — establish the multimodal Inspire library.
- ../../docs/hackathon-build/evidence/webmcp-live-collaboration-clean.png — prove the live connected Session, bounded Template receipt, one-slide revision, attribution, and Undo.
- ../../docs/hackathon-build/evidence/carousel-contact-sheet.png — show the finished seven-slide output.
- ../../public/template-studio/mountain-mist.png — visual texture already used by the product's Template Studio.

## Customizations

- Feature the site's own captured screens as the video's primary assets.
- Use clear callouts around the exact WebMCP actions: discover, connect, read bounded context, revise one slide, review, undo.
- Keep publishing outside the agent boundary and show that the creator owns final approval.

## Notes

- The video must be shorter than three minutes and contain audio.
- Do not claim a public deployment, public repository, or submitted Devpost entry until those exist.
- Do not imply that VibeStudio replaces Codex; it makes the creator's existing agent consistent over time.
- Avoid generic AI gradients, exaggerated claims, or simulated chat messages presented as real actions.
