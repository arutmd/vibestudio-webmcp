## Video direction

VibeStudio should feel like a calm, premium instrument rather than an AI spectacle. Use an ink-black field, warm paper surfaces, and coral only for agency, selection, and state change. Geist carries product language; Geist Mono carries receipts, tools, and version evidence. Motion is crisp and causal: an action changes one visible thing, then settles long enough to read. Preserve the real product screenshots as proof, frame them generously, and never fake interface behavior inside those images. The camera moves only to direct attention; it does not chase every control. Keep the creator and Codex visibly equal participants, while the final lockup gives the creator the last word.
